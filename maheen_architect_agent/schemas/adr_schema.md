# ADR Schema

## Purpose

Architecture Decision Records explain why important architecture decisions were made.

---

## Required Sections

### 1. ADR Metadata

- adr_id
- title
- status
- created_date
- related_blueprint_id

### 2. Context

What problem or situation led to this decision?

Required fields:

- problem
- constraints
- assumptions

### 3. Decision

What decision was made?

Required fields:

- selected_option
- reason_for_selection

### 4. Alternatives Considered

Other options that were considered.

Required fields:

- option_name
- reason_not_selected

### 5. Consequences

What are the trade-offs?

Required fields:

- positive_consequences
- negative_consequences
- risks

### 6. Traceability

Required fields:

- related_requirement_id
- related_feature_id
- related_component_id
